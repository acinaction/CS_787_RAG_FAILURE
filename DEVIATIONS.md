## Observations to keep in mind (no change made)
- Llama-2-chat answers start with a preamble ("Sure, I'd be happy to help! Based on Passage 1, …", ~13 tokens). It lowers token-F1
  (containment then decides most labels) and ReDeEP features are averaged over preamble tokens too. Prompt is frozen, so this is
  reported, not changed.
- C5 semantic plausibility for "what" questions is still imperfect without NER (see `c5_validation_v2.csv`; `human_ok` column for review).
